## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM: Authored, not category-interchangeable. Ledger grid, Oswald/Jost two-voice split, one amber signal, zero shadows, square corners, warm 85° hue carried into a real warm night theme, 404 and OG image. No AI-slop tells. The weakness is not slop: the system's restraint undercuts its readers. The faintest, smallest text is the keyword layer recruiters scan (9px muted stack tags), and the amber status light is spent on "Always learning", which is not a state.

Detector: CLI exit 0, 4 advisory `design-system-font-size` findings (not-found.tsx:21 clamp lower bound 3.5rem vs documented 2rem; 3 in opengraph-image.tsx). Browser overlay, home light: 116 findings — undersized-ui-text 93 (87 at 9px, 6 at 10px), low-contrast 15 (14× --text-muted 4.47:1, 1× amber text 2.3:1), numbered-section-labels 3, straggler 2 (flaky), skipped-heading 1, line-length 1 (~88ch), cream-palette 1. Home dark: low-contrast 15, all --text-muted at 3.4:1. 404: 6 findings incl. amber "404"/"Contact"/"←" at 2.3:1, 10px back link.

Agreement: contrast (muted text in dark, amber as text in light), missing h2 outline, project index numerals (LLM called them decorative by DESIGN.md's own rule; detector flagged numbered labels). False positives: cream-palette, hero-eyebrow-chip on 404, OG image font sizes (Satori image, not page text). undersized-ui-text is by-design per DESIGN.md caption/label-sm ramp, but both reviews agree the interactive 10px controls and the 9px tags are where the ramp fails real readers.

## Overall Impression

Strongest-crafted personal site category-wise: coherent, ownable, honest. The design is fine; the reading experience is not yet. Contrast and size fail exactly where recruiters look, the conversion action is silent on mobile, and the first viewport hides the current role. Biggest opportunity: make the ledger legible and the email row loud, without touching the aesthetic.

## What's Working

1. Disciplined, ownable system: ledger rows, two-voice type, one hue + one signal, carried into 404 and OG image; Cyrillic leading tuned per language (Hero.tsx:12).
2. Proof over claims executed: tags live inside entries; project technical panels are specific and verifiable (Lexical AST rebuild, symlink-flip deploys, honest "server owned by FINKI"); GitHub row points at this site's repo.
3. Plumbing: server-resolved theme/language cookies, aria-expanded/controls disclosures, working skip link, reduced motion honoured, no horizontal scroll at 320px.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Keyword layer and signal colour fail contrast.** --text-muted 4.47:1 light / 3.4:1 dark across ~149 elements; amber as text 2.3:1 on paper ("Always learning", active nav, hover, expanded toggle); amber focus ring 2.3:1, below 3:1 non-text minimum. 9px is the most common size (stack tags, column heads, years, contact labels). Why: tags are what recruiters pattern-match; in dark mode they nearly vanish. Fix: StackTag.tsx 9px→11px and --text-secondary; other 9px captions→10–11px; dark --text-muted 50%→~62% L; add light-theme `--accent-text` (~oklch(50% 0.12 75)) for amber text and focus ring, keep bright amber for dots/progress bar. Command: $impeccable audit, then $impeccable typeset.
2. **[P1] Email copy silent on mobile and for screen readers; failure fallback vanishes.** Indicator hidden below md, tooltip suppressed on touch, zero aria-live regions, aria-describedby points at a tooltip only rendered while visible, error state auto-clears after 2000ms. Why: the conversion moment looks broken. Fix: inline "Copied"/"Couldn't copy" text at all breakpoints, visually-hidden role="status" per row, error persists until blur/Escape, tooltip always rendered (hidden). Command: $impeccable harden.
3. **[P1] Six-second pass misses the current role.** Avenga appears mid-sentence in bio (en.ts:73); amber status slot says "Always learning"; md:min-h-dvh hero shows no Experience row in first viewport. Violates PRODUCT principle 4 and DESIGN Status Light Rule. Fix: status line "● Now: IT Operations & Support · Avenga / Engineer since 2024", or a caption under the title; let the Experience label peek above the fold. Command: $impeccable clarify.
4. **[P2] Macedonian parity breaks in visible places.** not-found.tsx English-only; "Light · Dark" and toggle aria-labels English; contact labels not in dictionary; MK philosophy written in 3 paragraphs but renders as one 946-char block (no whitespace-pre-line, Hero.tsx:69); "Review", "Associate Инженер…" half-translated. Fix: move strings into Dictionary, 404 reads useLanguage() via client leaf, add whitespace-pre-line. Command: $impeccable harden.
5. **[P2] Semantic outline and 404 nav broken.** No h2 (SectionLabel is a span), roles/programmes are <p>, h1 reads "GordanAleksandrovski" (no space between spans), current-role dot aria-label on bare span, no new-tab cue; 404 nav hrefs `#experience` dead, atBottom lights Contact. Fix: SectionLabel as <h2>, roles <h3>, sr-only space, role="img" on dot, `/#…` hrefs off home, skip active-section logic without sections. Command: $impeccable audit.
